📝 Important Formulas

Key formulas and shortcuts for quick reference

LCM & HCF

Product of two numbers = LCM × HCF

If a=12, b=18
HCF=6, LCM=36
12×18 = 36×6 = 216
Percentage

Percentage = (Value/Total) × 100

Percentage increase:
((New - Original)/Original) × 100
Time & Work

Work = Rate × Time

Combined work rate:
1/T = 1/T₁ + 1/T₂
Ratio & Proportion

If a:b = c:d, then a×d = b×c

Proportion: a/b = c/d
Cross multiplication: a×d = b×c
Profit & Loss

Profit % = (Profit/Cost) × 100

Selling Price = Cost × (1 + Profit%)
Cost Price = Selling Price / (1 + Profit%)
Simple Interest

SI = (P × R × T) / 100

P = Principal, R = Rate, T = Time
Amount = P + SI
